## Act VII: FAQ (Short, Honest, Creator-to-Creator)

**Headline**

## Straight answers before you roll.

### Will Sohizi magically make my character 100% identical in every shot?

No AI model on earth is magic every single roll—and anyone promising 100% one-click perfection is lying to you. What Sohizi does is cure **workflow amnesia**: your character sheets, approved frames, and lighting references stay locked to the shot, so you guide the model with visual anchors instead of starting from scratch.

### Will I burn through all my credits just trying to get one usable clip?

Most credit waste happens when tools force you to generate expensive video before you've locked the frame. In Sohizi, you lock your character look and storyboard stills first—then roll video only on the shots you've already approved.

### Does the AI take over my creative decisions?

Never. We hate "one-click movie generators" that spit out generic templates and ignore your camera directions. You control the script, the framing, the references, and the cut. Koli only steps in when you ask.
