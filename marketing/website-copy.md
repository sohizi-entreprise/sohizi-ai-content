## 3. Problem

**Headline**

## The hard part isn't making a clip. It's keeping the film together.

**Body**  
The script is in a document. Character references are in a folder. Storyboard frames came from one generator, motion from another, voice from a third, and the cut lives in an editor that has never seen the scene.

Every handoff creates more work. Download the file. Upload it again. Find the right reference. Paste the character description into another prompt. Work out which version you approved—and why.

The tools can make assets. They do not share a production. So every new shot risks forgetting the decisions the film has already made.

**Pull quote**

> Your production should not start from a blank prompt every time.

### What is Sohizi Lab?

Sohizi Lab is a project-aware AI video production workspace. You can write scripts, plan scenes and storyboards, generate images, video, voice, and music, organize project assets, and edit the final cut inside one project.

The current product is built for individual AI filmmakers and creators who handle several stages of production themselves. It is a fit for narrative shorts, music videos, cinematic ads, visual stories, and story-led YouTube work. Team collaboration is not part of the current release.

A generator primarily creates an asset from a prompt or reference. Sohizi Lab is the project around those generations: the script, scenes, shots, files, references, media, and edit. ## Copy rationale

### Why the hero changes

“Make your whole AI video in one tool. Not eight.” communicates consolidation, but competitors can make the same claim. The replacement makes the benefit specific to multi-shot work and introduces the project's memory as the differentiator.

The selected headline passes the “Now you can” test: now the creator can make the next shot with the context of the film already present.

### Why product proof comes second

The current page asks visitors to trust an abstract diagram and placeholder showcase. A real scene moving through real product states can prove the main promise before the page asks the visitor to read a detailed argument.

### Why the problem changes

The new problem section keeps the strongest part of the existing page—context loss—but describes the production objects creators actually manage: script, characters, references, storyboard frames, takes, voice, and edit.

### Why “all-in-one” becomes supporting copy

Sohizi does combine many tools, but market leaders do too. The connected project and project-aware assistant are harder to reduce to a feature checklist and more relevant to the customer's difficult job: finishing a coherent video.

### Why the audience narrows

The current release is designed for individuals and does not include team collaboration. Speaking primarily to solo AI filmmakers increases relevance and avoids attracting agencies with needs the product cannot yet meet.

### Why the page avoids perfect-consistency claims

Character and style consistency remain partly dependent on models, references, prompts, and iteration. Sohizi can credibly promise a better system for preserving and applying context. It should not guarantee a perfect output.
