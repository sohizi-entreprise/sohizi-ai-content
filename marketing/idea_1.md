**Section Headline:**
Five stages. One shared project brain.

### 1. Research, brainstorm, and shape the script

Don't stare at a blank page. Search the web for real-world inspiration, bounce plot twists around, and structure your screenplay or beat sheet with an AI creative partner that stays with you for the whole film.

- **[Illustration 1]:** _Ask the agent in chat to search the web for a story idea and use the `Plan` skill to turn it into a structured script._

### 2. Build your World Bible (Characters, Locations & Files)

Before you roll a single shot, organize your project folders and lock in the building blocks of your world. Generate character turnaround sheets, wardrobe references, and locations that stay pinned to your project—so your lead actor never turns into a stranger in Scene 2.

- **[Illustration 2]:** _Create a `/characters` folder in the project tree and generate a consistent character sheet._

### 3. Preview the film on a visual Storyboard

See how your story plays before spending expensive video credits. Turn your script into scene-by-scene storyboard frames, adjust camera angles, lighting, and pacing, and make sure the sequence works visually before moving to video.

- **[Illustration 3]:** _Generate and rearrange visual storyboard cards with scene lighting and camera specs._

### 4. Generate consistent Video, Images & Audio—two ways

Because your AI assistant already knows your script, folders, and character sheets, you can generate consistent shots, voices, and sound effects just by asking in chat. Want hands-on control over models, parameters, and seeds? Switch to the **Media Generation tab** anytime.

### 5. Cut the final film with an AI-powered Video Editor

No downloading 40 clips just to re-upload them into another editor. Your approved shots and audio sit right next to a multi-track timeline. Ask the AI agent to assemble a rough cut, trim pacing, or sync audio—then grab the timeline handles yourself for frame-accurate control.
