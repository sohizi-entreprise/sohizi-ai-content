## Problem and value proposition

Making a short film shouldn’t take nine tabs.

Your script is in one app. Your images are in another. Your voiceover lives somewhere else. Your hero’s face changes with every generation, and you re-explain your story to the AI every time you open a new window.

Sohizi Lab puts your entire project in one place, with one AI that has read all of it.
So you can stop managing files and get back to what you do best: creating and directing.
