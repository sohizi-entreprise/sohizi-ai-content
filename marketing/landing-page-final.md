## 09. FAQ

# Straight answers before you roll.

### Do I need filmmaking experience?

No. If you can describe a scene, you can start. Your AI partner handles the technical parts, and you can go as deep as you want wherever you want more control.

### What does it mean that the AI "knows my project"?

Your script, characters, style, and decisions live in one shared project. Every time you ask for something, the AI draws on all of it, so you never re-explain your story and your visuals stay connected from shot to shot.

### Will my character look identical in every shot?

No AI model is perfect on every generation, and anyone promising one-click perfection isn't being honest with you. What Sohizi does is keep your character sheets, approved frames, and references attached to every shot, so you guide the model with real visual anchors instead of starting from scratch each time.

### Will I burn through my credits?

Most credit waste happens when you generate expensive video before the shot is planned. In Sohizi, you lock your characters and storyboard frames first, then roll video only on shots you've already approved.

### Does the AI make creative decisions for me?

Never. You're always the director. The AI helps with research, planning, generation, and editing when you ask, and you decide what makes the final cut.

### Can I edit manually?

Always. Every AI action is editable, from the script and storyboard to the media tab and the timeline.
