## 08. FAQ

### What is Sohizi Lab?

Sohizi Lab is an AI-native creative studio that brings story development, project organization, storyboarding, media generation, and video editing into one connected workspace.

### Do I need filmmaking experience to use Sohizi Lab?

Sohizi Lab is designed to make the creative process more approachable. You can communicate your ideas in natural language and use your AI creative partner to help shape them, while keeping control over the direction.

### Can I edit my videos manually?

Yes. Sohizi Lab combines an AI-controlled video editor with direct editing controls, so you can guide your agent or make creative decisions yourself.

### How is Sohizi Lab different from other AI video generators?

Sohizi Lab focuses on the entire creative journey, not just individual generations. Your project context connects your story, characters, assets, and editing workflow, helping you build a cohesive film across multiple steps.

### Can I generate images, videos, and audio in the same project?

Yes. Sohizi Lab brings these media types into one project so you can create and organize assets without constantly moving between disconnected tools.

### Does the AI make creative decisions for me?

You're always the director. The AI helps with research, planning, generation, and editing, while you guide the vision and decide what makes the final cut.
