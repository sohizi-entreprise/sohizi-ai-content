1. A Day in the Life of a Coffee Bean This follows a bean from a Colombian farm through roasting and shipping to someone's morning cup. It's narrated in a warm voice and ends on the first sip. Shows: a timeline structure, a varied set of locations, and a product-ad feel that businesses recognize.

2. The Robot Who Learned to Paint A factory robot is thrown away and finds a paintbrush in a junkyard. It paints a sunset, and a child sees it. It's a simple arc with a heartwarming ending. Shows: a stylized, non-human main character that stays consistent. This is easier for AI than realistic people.

3. 48 Hours in Tokyo (A Travel Short) This is a fast, rhythmic montage: neon streets, ramen steam, a night train, a temple at dawn. Shows: a moodboard and palette, a quick plan-to-video flow, and a format every creator and brand wants.

4. Launch Day: A Sneaker Brand Ad This is a 30-second product spot with close-ups, motion, and a tagline at the end. Shows: commercial use. It's the story businesses will pay for, and it works well for the landing page.
